Hotel software built in Nigeria, for Nigerian hotels

HotelOpX is a hotel management platform built and operated from Lagos. It brings the front desk, restaurant and bar, housekeeping, accounts and staff records into a single system, so a hotel owner can see what is happening across the property without stitching together notebooks, spreadsheets and WhatsApp messages.

Why we built it

Most hotel software sold in Nigeria was designed somewhere else. It assumes reliable power, reliable internet, card-only payments and pricing in dollars. Hotels here work differently: the light goes, the network drops, guests pay by transfer or cash, and the bill has to balance at the end of a shift regardless.

HotelOpX is built around those realities. It keeps working when the connection goes and syncs once it returns. It records payments by card, transfer, cash or POS terminal against the right room and the right shift. It is priced in naira.

What the platform covers

  • Property management — reservations, check-in and check-out, room status, rate plans and folios.
  • Point of sale — restaurant, bar and room service, with QR ordering for guests and kitchen display screens.
  • Housekeeping — room assignment, cleaning status and inspection, visible to the front desk in real time.
  • Finance — daily revenue, expenditure, shift reconciliation and audit trails.
  • People — staff records, roles and permissions, shifts and payroll inputs.

How we work

We onboard each hotel directly rather than leaving owners to configure a system alone: rooms, rates, menus and staff are set up with you, and your team is trained on the parts they will actually use. Support is on WhatsApp, because that is where Nigerian hotel managers already are.
