Hotel management software built for Nigerian hotels

HotelOpX brings the front desk, restaurant and bar, housekeeping, accounts and staff into one platform, so you can see what is happening across your hotel as it happens — instead of piecing it together from notebooks, spreadsheets and WhatsApp messages.

Built for how hotels here actually run

It keeps working when the power and the network go, and syncs everything once they return. Guests pay by card, transfer, cash or POS terminal, and every payment lands against the right room and the right shift. Pricing is in naira.

What it covers

Property management for reservations, check-in and room status. Point of sale for the restaurant and bar, with QR ordering and kitchen screens. Housekeeping assignment and inspection. Finance, with daily revenue, expenditure and shift reconciliation. Staff records, roles and shifts.

Pricing

From ₦25,000 per month for small hotels and guest houses, ₦60,000 per month for mid-sized hotels with several revenue centres, and a tailored plan for chains. See full pricing.
